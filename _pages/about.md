Highly motivated researcher focused on applying my knowledge of deep-learning, LLMs, explainable AI, and multimodal
models to contribute to the field of human robot interaction, robotics and conversational AI.

Research interests 
======
My research focuses on Affective computing and applying psychological concepts to human-computer interaction. My PhD thesis explored the detection and generation of empathic behaviour in computers and robotic agents. I have worked on emotion detection and emotional behavior in robots. In particular, the generation of emotion-based behavior to improve human-robot interaction in social robots.
